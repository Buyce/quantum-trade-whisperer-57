## Revised investor narrative

1. **Built, connected, and trading** — replace the old “execution is still off” wording because the live controls are currently enabled. Explain that order placement is guarded by global, account, policy, exposure, confirmation, and emergency-stop controls.
2. **A promising first operating result, not a proven edge** — present positive broker money alongside the sub-50% win rate and slightly negative planned-risk R. The strength is the audited execution trail and ability to diagnose results, not a claim of statistical profitability.
3. **Why win rate alone is the wrong target** — explain that payoff distribution, expectancy in R, execution cost, drawdown, and sample independence determine whether the system has durable value.
4. **The improvement loop already built** — show the flow from broker evidence to execution-quality scoring, drawdown brakes, expected-R prioritisation, adaptive spread limits, replay, chronological holdout checks, and controlled rule changes.
5. **What is operational now** — broker-derived drawdown brakes; 33 execution-quality score rows; adaptive spread limits backed by 342 statistics rows; expected-R ranking at automatic enqueue; 109 filter comparisons; and eight chronological walk-forward checks.
6. **What is still under validation** — no walk-forward gate is currently confirmed, all five exit variants have zero usable path samples, and no active execution cooldown exists at this snapshot. These remain pipeline capabilities, not realised performance improvements.
7. **How outcomes may improve** — use a hypothesis-to-evidence roadmap rather than a numerical return forecast:
   - reduce preventable losses with drawdown brakes and execution-quality pauses;
   - reject expensive fills with adaptive spread limits;
   - allocate limited order capacity away from cohorts whose full confidence interval is below zero;
   - test exit variants on recorded paths before any live policy change;
   - promote only changes that pass sample, cluster, and chronological holdout gates.
8. **Investor takeaway** — P-Trades has moved beyond a scanner into an auditable trading control and research platform; the next milestone is proving repeatable positive expectancy, not merely increasing the headline win rate.
