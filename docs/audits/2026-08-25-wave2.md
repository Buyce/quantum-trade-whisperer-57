## 2. What was built

- **Asset-class registry.** `assetClass` (`fx | metal | energy | index`) and
  `priceUnit` (`pip | price | index_point`) on every definition; `XAGUSD`, `USOIL`,
  `UKOIL`, `NAS100` added with `contractSize`, `lotStep`, `minLot` and `spreadFloor`
  all `null`. `contractSpecs()` omits instruments with unknown geometry, so sizing
  refuses instead of guessing.
- **Price units.** Distance reporting in price units, broker points, broker ticks
  and spread-to-ATR, with pips restricted to FX and index points to indices. Null
  with a stated refusal whenever the broker unit is unknown.
- **Versioned calendars.** One per asset class, with weekly close, daily break,
  holidays, venue timezone and DST policy. `fx_spot`/`metal_spot` v1 reproduce the
  existing Wave 0 week byte-for-byte. `energy_cfd`/`us_index_cfd` v1 are
  `venue_local` and deliberately **unusable** until broker boundaries are sourced.
  A quote whose broker timestamp predates the current open window is refused.
- **Alias discovery.** Bounded provider-inventory read recording `candidate`,
  `ambiguous`, `missing`, `spec_unusable`, `trade_mode_unusable` or `error` into
  `instrument_alias_discovery`. It never writes a mapping or a specification.
- **Sampler.** Now records `asset_class`, reports `spreadPips` as null for non-FX,
  and refuses any instrument whose calendar is not usable — recorded as
  `calendarRefused` on the run rather than silently skipped.
- **Capacity projection.** Prices an activation per instrument per day with a retry
  reserve and refuses activation without headroom, so Wave 2 cannot crowd out Wave 0.
- **Correlation and news risk.** `metals_usd`, `energy`, `index_risk` groups with
  conservative grouped exposure; per-asset news families where unknown coverage
  suppresses new entries rather than clearing them.
- **Strategy portability manifests.** Per-asset assumption status; `energy` and
  `index` carry `invalid` entries and are wired to nothing.
- **Schema.** `asset_class` on spread samples/stats (existing rows classified),
  `instrument_alias_discovery`, `instrument_calendar_bindings`,
  `instrument_correlation_groups` — all admin-read, service-role-write — and four
  Wave 2 lifecycle rows at `disabled`.

## 3. What was deliberately NOT done

no candidates, no shadow enrolment, no signals, no alerts, no MCP exposure, no
execution, no change to `lifecycle_enforced`, no provenance backfill of the 2,732
legacy observation rows, and no change to Wave 0 or Wave 1 behaviour.
